title: "AI Marketing Campaign: Multi-Channel Content with Compliance Review"
category: "FlyRank"
status: "Completed"
problem: "Brand content work carries two real risks: inconsistent voice across channels, and unverified or exaggerated product claims, which create real compliance risk in regulated categories such as wellness and supplements. The need was a multi-channel campaign that stayed on-brand and compliance-safe without rebuilding the process for every asset."
approach: "Configured a Claude Project with a reusable brand brief and seven standing instructions covering voice, compliance, quality control against AI-cliché phrasing, integrity (no invented statistics, studies, or testimonials), platform-native adaptation, claim control, and regulatory control. Built one locked central campaign narrative and adapted it natively across a week of multi-channel content, then ran a dedicated claims-audit pass against the original brand material."
outcome: "The audit pass found that several claims in the original brand draft, including specific mineral-count, absorption, and regulatory wording claims, could not be substantiated from the supplied source material, and were neutralized, removed, or flagged for human verification rather than published as-is. This was a proposed assignment submission, not a live campaign: no publication, client approval, real performance data, or testimonials are claimed. The verifiable deliverable is a full reusable brand brief, seven standing instructions, one campaign narrative executed across multiple formats, and a real claims-audit pass that caught roughly nine distinct unsupported claims before they reached a draft."
