title: "Untapped Marketing Channels Strategy"
category: "FlyRank"
status: "Completed"
problem: "FlyRank had a strong foundation in organic and AI search growth (AEO), but also relied on saturated outreach models such as generic Meta Ads, cold email, and standard LinkedIn pitching, channels that B2B SaaS founders and marketing agency owners are increasingly immune to. Operating with a lean three-person team and a near-zero budget meant the wrong channel choice would be costly."
approach: "Used NotebookLM and Claude for source-grounded research, then evaluated four candidate channels against audience presence, effort, cost, and risk. Ranked them into a shortlist, and for each wrote a deep-dive covering why the audience is there, why it fits FlyRank, estimated effort and cost, real risks, and a concrete one-line test plan, with every draft manually reviewed before finalizing."
outcome: "A ranked report covering four channels: high-intent Slack and Discord communities, strategic co-marketing partnerships with non-competing SaaS tools, value-first Indie Hackers case studies, and Shopify App Ecosystem co-marketing. The core strategic recommendation was shifting from generic high-ticket outreach to a low-friction hook, a free AI Search Visibility Audit, as the entry point for high-intent conversations. This is a strategy and prioritization report; it has not yet been executed or measured, and no results are claimed."
